# Product

## Register

brand

## Users

Small business owners and founders who want their online presence to reflect serious quality — not just a functional site, but one that earns trust on first impression. They're looking for a studio that can articulate what "premium" looks like and deliver it without hand-holding. Decision-makers evaluating agencies; they scan fast, judge on feel, and go deeper only when something catches them.

## Product Purpose

Three Dimos (short for Three Dimensions) is a web studio that builds interactive business websites and landing pages combining design, code, and motion under one roof. The landing page is the product demo — every scroll, hover, and transition is proof of the craft being sold. Success means a prospect contacting the studio to start a project, convinced by the site itself.

## Brand Personality

Cinematic · precise · alive

Voice is collective studio "we" — confident, direct, no-fluff. The tagline "Design. Code. Motion." is the brand axis: three disciplines, one studio, nothing outsourced between them.

## Anti-references

Generic Awwwards fodder: over-animated dark-mode agencies that scroll-hijack, stack particle effects, and feel technically impressive but forgettable. Anything that impresses other designers but alienates normal business owners. The trap is visible craft — motion that shows off rather than communicates. Three Dimos aims to be cinematic without being alienating; alive without being chaotic.

## Design Principles

1. **The site is the pitch deck** — every hover state, transition, and interaction is a silent demonstration of what the studio sells. A flaccid element is a broken argument.
2. **Motion earns its place** — animation is structural (guides attention, reveals hierarchy, signals interactivity), never decorative filler. Three Dimos sells motion; how it moves must be its best argument.
3. **Cinematic without exclusion** — the dark, high-energy aesthetic must remain legible and trustworthy to a small business owner, not just impressive to a designer. Craft serves the audience, not the portfolio.
4. **Specificity over spectacle** — avoid the Awwwards formula. The signature is in the precise details: the letter-spacing, the easing curve, the exact moment a reveal fires. Beauty through intention, not accumulation.
5. **One studio, no seams** — the brand story (Design + Code + Motion under one roof) must feel structurally true in the page itself. Nothing should feel borrowed, templated, or inconsistent between sections.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

WCAG 2.1 AA. Reduced motion already implemented via `prefers-reduced-motion` and a `--motion` CSS variable that scales animation intensity to zero. Keyboard navigation required throughout. Body text contrast ≥ 4.5:1 against background.
